# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in CutSteward welcoming and harassment-free,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give actionable feedback, assume good faith while checking
evidence, and protect other people's privacy and credentials. Harassment,
threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and publication of private material
are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile or private security-reporting channel. Maintainers may edit, remove, or
reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when conduct threatens the community or project.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
